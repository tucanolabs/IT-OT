# IT/OT Monday Kit

**The technology converged. The teams didn't.**



This is a free starter kit for aligning IT and OT security governance when you don't have the budget, the team or the tools yet. Everything here is editable. Download it, change it to fit your sites, and use it.

It accompanies the talk *Roadmap for Aligning IT/OT Governance with Evolving Global Regulatory Mandates*.

## 

## What's in the kit

|#|File|What it does|Time to use|
|-|-|-|-|
|1|`01-Self-Assessment.xlsx`|15 yes/no statements that place you on the four-level maturity roadmap (Fragmented, Aligned, Integrated, Resilient) and tell you the next step.|15 minutes|
|2|`02-Control-Crosswalk.xlsx`|Map once, comply many. 30 starter controls in 8 families, mapped against every mandate you face, with a status summary.|A few hours for one site|
|3|`03-90-Day-Starter-Plan.xlsx`|20 week-by-week actions that need no new budget or tools, with owners, effort and progress tracking.|90 days, a few hours a week|
|4|`04-IT-OT-Charter-Template.docx`|A one-page charter: who owns what, who decides what, how the teams meet, and shared measures.|One meeting|
|5|`05-Tabletop-Exercise.docx`|A ready-to-run 60-minute exercise for IT, OT, operations and safety, with an after-action record.|60 minutes|
|6|`06-Business-Case-One-Pager.docx`|A template for asking for budget in uptime, safety and plant-calendar terms, with worked examples.|An afternoon|

## 

## Where to start

1. **Week 1:** Get the IT security, OT/engineering and safety leads in one room. Run the **self-assessment** together.
2. **Week 2:** Agree the **charter** and name one accountable executive.
3. **Weeks 3 to 13:** Work through the **90-day plan**. It points you to the **crosswalk**, the **tabletop exercise** and the **business case** at the right moments.

If you only do three things: know where you are (1), map your obligations once (2), and get the teams in a room together (5).

## 

## Three things to do on Monday

1. Name one accountable executive for IT and OT security risk, with a joint charter.
2. Build one control map and crosswalk every mandate you face to it.
3. Get on the plant's calendar, and put security in the next capex plan and outage scope.

## 

## A note on safety

Any action that touches live OT systems, such as changing passwords, disabling remote access or testing a restore, should go through your normal change control and be approved by the people responsible for the process.
